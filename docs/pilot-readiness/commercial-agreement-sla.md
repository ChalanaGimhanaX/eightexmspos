## 2. Service Level Agreement (SLA) & Support Terms

### 2.1 Severity Levels and Response Targets

| Severity Level | Definition | Target First Response | Target Resolution |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Severity 1 (Critical)** | Complete billing lockout, database corruption, or inability to issue receipts across all counters. | **< 30 minutes** | **< 3 hours** (remote or on-site dispatch) |
| **Severity 2 (Major)** | Single counter down (other counters operating), cloud sync delayed, or printer communication error. | **< 1 hour** | **< 6 hours** |
| **Severity 3 (Minor)** | UI cosmetic questions, report formatting queries, or new staff training request. | **< 4 hours** | **Next business day** |

### 2.2 Support Channels
- **Hotline Phone Support**: Monday – Sunday: 7:30 AM – 9:30 PM (Asia/Colombo)
- **WhatsApp / Remote Desk Support**: Anydesk / RustDesk remote assistance available on demand.
- **On-Site Technical Dispatch**: Western Province (Colombo, Gampaha, Kalutara) within 3 hours. Other provinces scheduled within 24 hours.

---

## 3. Data Protection, Backup & Privacy Guarantee

1. **Local Autonomy**: All transaction data is stored locally in SQLite on each counter and operates continuously during internet or cloud outages.
2. **Encrypted Cloud Backups**: Daily database snapshots are encrypted with AES-256 before transmission and stored on secure cloud servers with 30-day versioned retention.
3. **Tenant Privacy**: Strict tenant isolation prevents any cross-business data access. Provider staff access requires explicit temporary authorization from the store owner.
